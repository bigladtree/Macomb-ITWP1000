Welcome to the Habitat!

"Are you wearing a frown? Life got you down? Sighing, Crying, Sulking through town? Your solace is here...In the habitat!"

Smile for Me is a video game created by Limbolane, an indie development company run by Day Lane and Yugo Limbo. It was released on May 31st, 2019, and uses a creative "nod-and-shake" mechanic as its main form of gameplay! Smile For Me is a game about making people happy! A direct quote from their website, "You may be silent, but you’ve got a lot to say! Nod-and-shake your way through the Habitat, with a host of funky characters chock-full of juicy gossip. Learn their quirks and qualms, and cure them of their blues!" Make people happy, befriend them, learn secrets, and more in Smile For Me!

This webpage will dive into the characters of Dr Habit, the overseer of the Habitat, and his (ex)assistant Kamal Bora. It will delve deep into the characters themselves, their motives, their history; all the juicy details! Alongside this, we will also explore The Big Event, an event promised by Dr Habit to all the habititans, and the illustrious Martha. What is "The Big Event"? Who is Dr Habit really? Why isn't Kamal is assistant anymore, and why does he seem so fearful? Find these questions out through this webpage! Enjoy meeting the characters and learning their history and secrets!
