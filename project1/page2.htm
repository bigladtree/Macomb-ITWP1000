Backstory

Dr. Habit is one of the most fleshed out characters in Smile For Me, to the point most of the other characters seen in the game don't have nearly as much known about them or their history. Every character has their own story and some history that are known, but none are as interesting as Kamal Bora. He is shown to be very close to Dr. Habit, being his dental assistant all the way up to a rainstorm which left Marthas (check The Big Event for information on her) teeth rusted. The rust resulted in Dr. Habit panicking and stealing everyones toothbrush to clean it, which resulted in Kamals teeth changing color due to his inability to brush his teeth. Their partnership ended when, "long story short", Dr. Habit mocked Kamal for his yellow teeth which were a direct result of his actions.

Relation to The Habitat

As stated earlier, he was Dr. Habits personal assistant. It is unknown how this relationship came to be, or why Kamal decided to stay in the Habitat after he quit. It is not known whether their partnership started before or after the creation of the Habitat. Kamals importance to the story, though, is that he allows you into Habit's office. He knows all the passcodes and passages throughout the Habitat, and allows the player entrance into the final area. Once ready, the player is meant to go to him to signify that they are done with everything they want to do before ending the game, Kamal telling the player there's no going back once he helps them unlock the door and that they should get their affairs in order before going to him. Many think Kamal stays at the Habitat due to his care for Dr. Habit, and how he'll only not show in the ending if you get the good ending, which results in Dr. Habit not seeing the error of his ways and you making him happy. In the other endings he's there to say "that could've gone better, I think.", showing guilt in the bad ending which results in his assumed death.
