Day Lane


Producer, Programmer, Gameplay Designer

Day is the primary programmer/game designer behind the game Smile For Me, aswell as LimboLane as a whole. She has helped create various published board and tabletop games. She also led the development of co-op mobile game Family Style, a game in which you and up to eight friends can scramble to prepare dishes, passing ingredients from phone to phone as you race against the clock to prepare as much as possible.

  • Check out Day Lane

Yugo Limbo


Animator, Developer, Comic Artist

The whole art style behind Smile For Me, as well as other games created/being created by LimboLane such as Face Love and Great God Grove, is all thanks to Yugo Limbo. Personal works of hers includes a 500 page psychedelic horror comic titled Be Kind, My Neighbor. This story follows lovers Wegg and Mr. Neighbor through their relationship as ritualistic, repeated murders continue to arise within the town they reside in.

LimboLanes Games

Check out their collection of games below:

FACE LOVE! Valentines is coming up, and all the citizens faces are gone! Use your own creativity to create faces for the citizens, and keep your face-factory up and running!

Smile For Me! The main star of the show at LimboLane. Smile For Me is all about making people happy! The Habitat is full of all sad people, and you have the ability to fix that! Nod and Shake your way through interactions, aswell as help the Habititans through their needs in order to cheer them up!

Great God Grove! GGG is LimboLanes current project, as it is not yet released. Way up in the mountains, deities and their worshippers lived peacefully before the pantheons King began to spread misinformation, causing the peace to be disrupted by bickering and fighting. As the mail carrier for The Grove, its your job to set the story straight through creatively delivering messages.
