Above is a slideshow of every character in the game Smile for Me (with the exceptions of The Bed, the player (who is officially called Flower Kid by the characters), and Dr. Habits parents, who are shown very briefly in a hidden slideshow. There are 25 characters total in the game not counting these ones. In Smile For Me, as stated before, your mission is to make people happy! These characters, called "Habititians" are at the Habitat for one reason and one reason only; they're sad. All of Smile for Me has you do some sort of task for the characters in order to cheer them up (with the exception of Nat Vancy, who is the tutorial character that shows you what cheered up characters look like.) Some of these tasks may include opening a jar of pickles for them (Randy Hapukurk), bonking poor old Ronbo in the head with a baseball (Millie Coulro), or helping rekindle dreams of becoming a famous singer that were given up on (Lulia Fame). All characters are unique and have a shining personality; no character is similar to another one. They all have their own reasons for being at the Habitat and they are the true charm and heart of Smile for Me. The fact that every character is so unique in their own way allows Smile for Me to shine and make its presence different from other indie games.

You may have noticed thst some characters names are quite odd. This is because they're puns! in Alphabetical order: Lulia Fame references her dreams of becoming a famous singer, evident in the last name Fame. Mirphy Fotoparat is a photographer, referenced in her last name having "Foto". Nat Vancy's last name references the word vampire, as her and her father are both vampires. Parsley Botch's first name is a reference to the herb Parsley, as his quest line involves you getting him his dads food. Trencil Varnnia is a pun referencing Transylvania, as Trencil and his daughter Nat are both vampires, and finally Wallus Breadbear's first name is a reference to the fact that this character is never actually shown, as he lives in the walls of the Habitat.

Below goes more in depth on a few of the supporting characters not previously gone over: The Carnival Attendants, Wallus, and Tiff Webber.


The Carnival Attendants

The Carnival Attendants are probably the most mysterious and unique characters in the game. There are three of them total, each one running an attraction in the Carnival; the Button Press game, the Fortune Teller booth, and the "Wack a Molar" game. I decided to give them a mention due to their strange relations to Dr. Habit. They were created by Dr. Habit for starters, evident in them all calling him "Father" or "Dad". This also shows in the fact that they are all made of paper. They also know a lot about Dr. Habit, stating things like "Father hasn't been happy in a long, long time" and knowing his history. They also are key to getting the good ending, as one of them holds a diary entry, one hints at Dr. Habits story upon being shown any diary entries, and one unlocks a key area in the game. How they came to be created is unknown. These entities are also commonly referred to as "Carla"

Wallus Breadbear

Wallus is one of four known workers for Dr. Habit, the other three being Tiff, Gillis and Kamal. Wallus was a janitor for Dr. Habit, before, like Kamal, being so humiliated they decided to quit. For Wallace, his experience with Dr. Habit was so humiliating he decided to live in the wall out of pure shame, not wishing to show his face at all. It is unsaid what this event was. Wallus is very important to the game due to him unlocking the final area: the Doctors office, you unlocking it through using a flash camera to take his photo for a Janitor ID card. It is implied that Wallus has been in the walls for a very long time, as showing him any polaroid of any other character in the game will result in him trying to talk to you in a friendly manner as though you were them (him thinking the polaroids are the person pictured, possibly due to the poor lighting)

Tiff Webber

Tiff Webber is a character found in the Lounge area of the game. She is a professional singer, being at the Habitat due to a contract with Dr. Habit. She wishes to leave the Habitat, but states that she can't, since every time she tries to cut the contract off, Dr. Habit "bursts into tears". She spends her time in the Lounge singing songs like "Eeny, Meeny, Miny, Molar" and "The Teeth on The Bus", which are just rewritten nursery rhymes to be teeth related. In destroying her contract for her (located in her room, which is the only character room to be shown), she becomes happy. While she isn't as important as Wallus or The Carnival Assistants, as she doesn't unlock anything for the player, I thought to include her due to the fact that she is purely at the Habitat out of a contract, and not due to being unhappy as most other Habititans. While she doesn't directly unlock anything, an item found in her room is used to cheer up Ronbo, who then unlocks the Carnival for you.
