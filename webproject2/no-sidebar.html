Dr. Habit's PSAs

The perfect thing to wake up in the morning to!

Throughout the game Smile For Me, You will find yourself watching various PSA's that go in hand with various events in the game. Some of these PSA's are more important than others, this being indicated by their use of the Habit Puppet. You can achieve these more important PSA's through the introduction, sleeping for the first time, unlocking the Carnival, Lounge and Terrace, and on the day of the big event. These PSA's will take up the whole screen and automatically play for the player upon sleeping after the various Pre-Resiquites are achieved. The less important PSA's that'll occur every day without the use of Habits' voice show a static background, usually flowers of some sort, and have some sort of backhanded text appear. An example of one of them, shown on Day 8, can be read below:

HOW-DEE!
THAT'S "HELLO" IN BED-SPEAK.
DID U KNO?: WHEN YOU DISLIKE A WACK-KEY NEIGHBOR, NEVER B DIRECT! INSTEAD, TELL EVERY-1 HOW MEAN THEY ARE!
IT'S CALLED "N EGGING" AND THEY'LL WILL LOVE IT!
TOODLE-LOO PARD-NER!

There is one more category of PSA, that is shown as a sort of easter egg. These PSA's, titled "Dr. Habits Bedtime Stories, For Insomniacs and Bad Children." These PSA's a re only achieveable if the player decides to stay awake past the set curfew for the Habitat, which'll cause them to black out and for the PSA to appear on screen. There are five total of these PSA's before they begin to repeat themselves. These PSA's are closely related to the non-important ones you can find on the tv, with the exception that the player is forced to watch them. The background image of these PSA's have unsettling imagery, such as a dark lit street that keeps panickly panning to the left, or a dimly lit room focusing on the crack of the door, which only leads to darkness. An example of the first video in the set can be read below:

YOU'RE UP PAST CURFEW AREN'T YOU, LITTLE HABITICIAN.
MAY-BE YOU NEED A LITTLE BED-TIME-STORY.
LOOK DOWN THERE.

CAN YOU SEE THAT?
DO YOU KNOW WHAT THAT IS?

I KNOW WHAT THAT IS. :-)
KEEP LOOKING AND YOU'LL SEE IT.

I'M IN CONTROL HERE
DON'T FORGET IT.

Static PSA's (OLD CONCEPT)

Shown on the television every day without sound. This image example is unique in that, although it is how the Static PSA's look, this is an old concept for them. Dr. Habit's speech patterns were originally going to be dark and poetic until they turned into the iconic misspelling/Gibberish he has now, and this is an old concept of that idea. By clicking on the image, you can view these Old Concept PSA's. They're very similar to the current Static Ones recording wise, the key difference being the speech patterns and music (The normal Static PSAs are shown without sound). By clicking on the youtube video shown at the start of this page, you can view the Main and Normal Side PSA's.

Bedtime Stories

These PSA's are achieved through staying up past curfew and "blacking out", the player being forced to watch these upon doing so. Clicking on the image above will allow you to view these PSA's.
