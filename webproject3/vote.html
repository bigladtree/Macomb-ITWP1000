Voting Page

This page is meant for you to be able to vote on pictures.

Below is the form to fill out on this months top 3. By choosing one in each category, you are nominating that photo for first place. The votes at the end of the month will be tallied to determine which photos in each category earned first, second, and third place. You can view the final/standing results through the winners page, which will reflect current results/final results. These results are updated at the end of the day each day till the end of the month, where the winner is truly decided.

About

our website is a safe space for people to be able to get their pictures out to the world. Under three separate categories, people can post whatever they think the world will enjoy, and others can vote on them. At the end of each month, winners in each categories are decided via this voting system. Second and Third place also get honorable mentions.
