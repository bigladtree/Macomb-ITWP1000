Categories List

A compiled explanation of our websites various categories.

There are three categories you can choose to post in here at thePhotoBomb.com. These categories can be read about below:

Category 1: Concert Photos

In todays day and age, music is everywhere, able to be enjoyed by everyone. There's so many different types of music, it's practically impossible for a single person to not enjoy at least one genre. We encourage you to post all things related to concerts! Whether it be symphonic, a choir, a pop concert, your favorite singer, a death metal concert, a rave; all things that can be categorized as a concert is encouraged in this category!

Category 2: Plant Photos

Plants are everywhere; they keep our very planet alive! There are so many different types, and so many of them are so useful! Trees provide us with so many materials, flowers provide us with dyes and visual pleasure, and every single one provides us and every other living organism with oxygen! We encourage all sorts of gardening photos in this category, so feel free to take photos of various greenery you come across, or maybe even grow your own!

Category 3: Landscape Photos

Our earth is so beautiful in every way shape and form, and we encourage you to go out and share its beauty! In this category, any sort of landscape, manmade or natural, is encouraged. Feel free to take photos of great mountains you came across travelling, or simply a particularly beautiful spot in your backyard. Beauty is everywhere on our fine planet!

Rules

In order to post on thePhotoBomb.com, you must read the rules below and follow them to avoid your pictures possibly being taken down:

Rule #1

All photos posted must be in .jpg, .png, or .gif format

Rule #2

Explicit content is not allowed, such as nudity, violence, or other such things.

Rule #3

No posting stolen or AI generated photos of any kind

Rule #4

No posting photos unrelated to the categories listed.

Rule #5

No spam posting images.

About

our website is a safe space for people to be able to get their pictures out to the world. Under three separate categories, people can post whatever they think the world will enjoy, and others can vote on them. At the end of each month, winners in each categories are decided via this voting system. Second and Third place also get honorable mentions.
